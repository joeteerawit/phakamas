# Thai Spelling

Public, login-free lessons that teach Grade 2 students one Thai final-consonant group (มาตราตัวสะกด) per Lesson.

## Language

**Lesson** (บทเรียน):
A complete course for one final-consonant group, e.g. แม่กง.
_Avoid_: Course, module

**Step** (ขั้น):
One of the ordered stages of a Lesson (แม่กง has 10).
_Avoid_: Page, หน้า, screen

**Activity** (กิจกรรม):
A Step that produces a score: word selection, word filling, rhyme matching.
_Avoid_: Game, quiz, exercise

**Session**:
One student's run through a Lesson in one browser; a browser holds at most one Session.
_Avoid_: Attempt, visit

**Target Word** (คำในมาตรา):
A word whose every syllable ends in the Lesson's final consonant, e.g. ลิง, ผึ้ง; for แม่ ก กา, a word whose every syllable has no final consonant, e.g. ปู, ทะเล.
_Avoid_: Correct word, key word

**Supporting Word** (คำประกอบ):
A word used in a Lesson that is not wholly in its final-consonant group, e.g. งู, เงา (ง is their initial, not their final).
_Avoid_: Distractor (that is a wrong choice inside an Activity)

**Star** (ดาว):
The reward a student earns in an Activity according to their score; the Lesson's Stars add up on the summary Step.
_Avoid_: Point, coin, badge

**Mascot**:
The one character that guides the student through every Step of a Lesson; each Lesson has its own, e.g. น้องลิง for แม่กง, น้องส้ม for แม่กม.
_Avoid_: Character, owl, teacher avatar

**Theme** (ธีม):
The look of one Lesson: its Scenes, Mascot, colours and how each Step is laid out. No two Lessons share a Theme.
_Avoid_: Skin, style

**Word Catalog** (คลังคำ):
Every word the site knows, each with its picture, sound, spelling and final-consonant group, shared by all Lessons; a Session draws its Lesson's words from it at random.
_Avoid_: Word Bank (that is the box of words to drag on the word-filling Step), dictionary, db

**Scene** (ฉาก):
The moving picture behind a Step's words and buttons. Its objects come from the Lesson's words, but backdrop objects may be any word.
_Avoid_: Background, wallpaper
